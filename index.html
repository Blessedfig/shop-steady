What's a Stube?

Stube (SHTOO-beh): the cozy back room where the crew gathers

In the German towns that settled Wisconsin, the Stube was the warm room in the back of the tavern or the family home. It's where the regulars sat, the owner did the books after close, and the family talked over tomorrow's plans.

Downtown Stube is that room for your business. It's the one place your team checks before open, where the schedule, the checklists, the stock and the numbers all live, so everyone knows where to look.

"Check the Stube before you open."

A good house rule for any shop

Questions

Good questions, straight answers

Does it replace my point-of-sale system?

No. Keep ringing up sales in Shopify POS, Square or whatever you use. Downtown Stube works alongside it for the staff, stock and owner side. A Shopify connection is in progress so sales and stock flow in on their own.

How much does it cost?

Downtown Stube is free while it's in testing. After that it will stay priced for small shops, and early testers will hear the price first. Ask for early access.

Do my staff need training?

Very little. Each screen shows one clear next step, and checklists walk people through the routine as they work. Most people are comfortable after their first shift.

My business isn't a bookstore. Will it fit?

Yes. Pick retail, café, services or rentals during setup. Role names, modules and checklists all change to match, and you can rename anything.

Does it work on phones?

Yes. It runs in any modern browser and installs on iPhone, Android and computers. See how to install it.

Is the demo safe to try?

Yes. The demo uses made-up sample data that stays in your own browser. Please don't type real customer or staff details into it.

See it working in two minutes

Open the demo, switch "View as" between owner, manager and staff, and click around. Want it for your own shop? Email owner@theblessedfigtree.com for early access.
