The schedule lives in a group text, the opening routine lives in your head, and reorders live on five supplier websites. Shop Steady pulls it into one calm place.

Better numbers

Open the doors

Where it comes from

Built inside a real shop, not a boardroom

Shop Steady started at The Blessed Fig Tree, an independent bookstore in Antigo, Wisconsin. Every feature answers a real problem from the shop floor: the new hire who didn't know the closing routine, the Saturday nobody covered, the reorder that took a whole afternoon across five supplier sites.

It's built on a design system made for people working tired, busy shifts: large tap targets, plain words and one clear next step on every screen.

Questions

Good questions, straight answers

Does it replace my point-of-sale system?

No. Keep ringing up sales in Shopify POS, Square or whatever you use. Shop Steady works alongside it for the staff, stock and owner side. A Shopify connection is in progress so sales and stock flow in on their own.

How much does it cost?

Shop Steady is free while it's in testing. Pricing for other shops will be set before it opens more widely, and early testers will hear first.

Do my staff need training?

Very little. Each screen shows one clear next step, and checklists walk people through the routine as they work. Most people are comfortable after their first shift.

My business isn't a bookstore. Will it fit?

Yes. Pick retail, café, services or rentals during setup. Role names, modules and checklists all change to match, and you can rename anything.

Does it work on phones?

Yes. It runs in any modern browser and installs on iPhone, Android and computers. See how to install it.

Is the demo safe to try?

Yes. The demo uses made-up sample data that stays in your own browser. Please don't type real customer or staff details into it.

See it working in two minutes

Open the demo, switch "View as" between owner, manager and staff, and click around. Nothing to install.
