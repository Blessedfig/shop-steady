See how one change ripples out

What a $1 raise really costs, and what it takes to cover it

A raise, a hire, an order or a price change touches payroll taxes, insurance, cash, margin and your tax bill all at once. Move the sliders and watch it ripple. In the app, these start from your own pay rates, sales and margin.

Pick a change and move the sliders

These sliders need JavaScript. Example: a $1 raise for one person working 25 hours a week, 50 weeks a year, costs the store about $1,358 a year with payroll taxes and workers' comp, and about $964 after the tax write-off.

Sample numbers for a small Wisconsin bookstore. Estimates for learning, not tax advice: 2026 federal and Wisconsin figures, and your accountant has the final word.
