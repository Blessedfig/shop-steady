Setup, roles and sign-in

Downtown Stube shapes itself to your business, not the other way around.

Business types

Bookstore, retail shop, café, services or rentals. Each sets role names and starting modules for that location.

Four role tiers

Staff, shift lead, manager and owner. Rename any role, and choose which modules each one can open.

Invite-only sign-in

Password or an emailed code, stays signed in on personal devices, signs out on its own when idle.

Phones and computers

Installs on iPhone, Android and desktop. How to install.

Multiple locations

Each location keeps its own business type, role names and modules.

Accessible

Checked against WCAG 2.2 AA on every change. Our accessibility notes.
