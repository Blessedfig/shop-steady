Owner numbers

Know how the shop really did this week

Your register knows what sold. Downtown Stube adds what it cost: item costs, labor and the bills. Then it tells you what was left over, in plain words.

  • Monday money summary: sales, item costs, gross margin, labor as a share of sales, and what was left
  • Home dashboard: who's on shift, what's low, and what needs you today
  • Decision planner: before you hire, change hours or make a big purchase, see the monthly cost, how much more you'd need to sell, and how long it takes to pay back, using your own sales and pay rates
  • Ask: a built-in assistant that answers from your store's records, and only shows what the asker's role may see
  • Community impact report In testing: events, local jobs and local spending, ready for grants, landlords and the city

People and pay

From the schedule to the paycheck

Build the week, let the store tablet run the clock, and hand payroll a clean file. Wisconsin and federal rules are built in, so you don't have to remember them.

  • Schedule: publish the week, post open shifts, and get warned about short rest or late notice
  • Store mode: one shared tablet, a personal PIN for each person
  • Front desk: a simple home screen with big buttons for everyday jobs, so new hires are useful on day one

Time clock that follows the rules

Overtime counted per workweek, breaks timed (in Wisconsin, breaks under 30 minutes stay paid), and limits for workers under 16. Managers can fix a missed punch, and every fix is logged.

In testing

Payroll from the time clock

Each pay run adds up hours, overtime and gross pay, then hands a file to Gusto or QuickBooks Payroll for the taxes and deposits. Nothing sensitive like bank numbers is stored here.

Available now

HR files

Offer letters, tax forms and reviews, kept privately for each person. Only managers and the owner can open them.

Checklists and training

Checklists and courses that train as they go

Opening, closing and daily routines, written once by you and followed step by step by everyone. New staff learn the job by doing it, and you can see what got done.

  • Separate lists for opening, closing and any day of the week
  • Each step can carry a short how-to in your own words
  • Training: ready-made courses for every job (front desk, special orders, customer service, opening and closing, safety, the register), which you can edit, hide or add to
  • Store policies: your own answers to common questions, in one course

Stock and ordering

Reorder what sold, from the right supplier

Your stock, your vendors and your costs in one place. Downtown Stube drafts the orders. You check them and send.

Available now

Inventory and counts

In testing

In testing

Vendors and title catalog

Every supplier's cost, discount and stock for a title, side by side, so you see your margin before you order. Staff see stock and prices, not costs.

In testing

Special orders

Quoted at your target margin, with deposits, a pickup message when the order lands, and a 30-day hold shelf.

In testing

Shopify connection

Sales and stock come in from Shopify on their own, and cleaned-up product details go back.

In testing

Product cleanup

Finds missing ISBNs, wrong costs from wholesale imports and missing details. You approve each fix, or let a rule run on its own.

Selling online too? A set of rules keeps only in-stock, ready-to-ship items in your Shopify online store, so customers can't order what you don't have.

Setup

Setup, roles and sign-in

Downtown Stube shapes itself to your business, not the other way around.

Business types

Bookstore, retail shop, café, services or rentals. Each sets role names and starting modules for that location.

Four role tiers

Staff, shift lead, manager and owner. Rename any role, and choose which modules each one can open.

Invite-only sign-in

Password or an emailed code, stays signed in on personal devices, signs out on its own when idle.

Phones and computers

Installs on iPhone, Android and desktop. How to install.

Multiple locations

Each location keeps its own business type, role names and modules.

Accessible

Checked against WCAG 2.2 AA on every change. Our accessibility notes.
