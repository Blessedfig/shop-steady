How we keep your shop's records safe

Downtown Stube holds your schedules, time clock, customers, special orders and cash logs. Here is how that information is protected, in plain words, and what isn't finished yet.

Who can get in

  • Sign-in is by invitation only. Nobody can create an account on their own.
  • Each person has a level: staff, shift lead, manager or owner. Pay, cash safe records, HR files and manager notes are kept in private areas that only a store manager or the owner can open. Staff phones never download them.
  • Store-mode PINs are checked by the database, not the tablet, and lock after repeated wrong tries.
  • The app signs you out after 12 hours without use.

Where the data lives

  • Your records are stored with Supabase, a database host that holds a SOC 2 Type 2 report. Data is encrypted while it travels (TLS) and while it is stored (AES-256). Downtown Stube itself is a small company and does not have its own SOC 2 audit.
  • We never take card payments. Those stay in your register (Shopify POS or similar).
  • We ask you not to store Social Security numbers, ID scans, bank account numbers or medical records in the app. Those belong in a locked file or your payroll provider. Keeping them out means a leak could never expose them.
  • We don't use fingerprint or face scans for clocking in.

The AI helpers

  • The Ask assistant only reads. It sees what the person asking is allowed to see, and nothing more. The owner sets a daily question limit.
  • When the app reads an invoice for you, it removes email addresses, phone numbers and card-like numbers before the text is sent. A person checks every line before anything counts.
  • AI-polished notes show your original next to the new wording, and you choose which to keep.

Your data is yours

  • The owner can download everything in one file at any time, from Setup. No request, no fee.
  • Cancel any time. There is no contract.
  • If Downtown Stube ever shuts down, we will give at least 90 days' notice so you can download everything first.

If something goes wrong

  • If we learn that your records were exposed, we will tell you as soon as we can, and no later than Wisconsin's 45-day limit for notifying affected people.
  • To report a security problem, email the address under Contact on our home page.

Not done yet

Downtown Stube is in private testing. Before other shops join, we are finishing:

  • Two-step sign-in (an authenticator app) required for owners and managers.
  • Daily hosted database backups on paid hosting, in addition to the owner's own download.
  • A public status page.
  • Terms of service and a data processing agreement reviewed by a lawyer.

Please don't enter real customer data in the demo.
